    常見問題FAQ

    關於罐子、成分，以及和 TzuSpark 合作的簡短回答。Quick answers about the cans, the ingredients, and working with TzuSpark.

    TzuSpark 是什麼？What is TzuSpark?

    TzuSpark 是個人創立的全天然氣泡汽水。四種口味是薑汁、蔓越莓薑汁、萊姆與黑櫻桃。TzuSpark is a personally created line of all-natural sparkling sodas. The four flavors are Ginger Ale, Cranberry Ginger Ale, Lime, and Black Cherry.

    口味有什麼不同？How do the flavors differ?

    薑汁溫暖、帶柑橘。蔓越莓薑汁偏酸。萊姆清新乾淨。黑櫻桃熟成順口。每一款都有氣泡。Ginger Ale is warm and citrusy. Cranberry Ginger Ale is tart. Lime is zesty and clean. Black Cherry is ripe and smooth. Every one of them is sparkling.

    有代工或自有品牌嗎？Do you offer OEM or private label?

    有。夥伴可以和我們一起調配方，把自己的名字放上罐子，並做小批量充填與包裝。Yes. Partners can shape a recipe with us, put their own name on the can, and have small batches filled and packed.

    罐子裡有什麼？What is in a can?

    氣泡水、蔗糖、天然香料與檸檬酸。TzuSpark 不使用人工色素。Carbonated water, cane sugar, natural flavors, and citric acid. TzuSpark does not use artificial colors.
