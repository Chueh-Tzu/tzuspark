    FAQ

    Quick answers about the cans, the ingredients, and working with TzuSpark.

    What is TzuSpark?

    TzuSpark is a personally created line of all-natural sparkling sodas. The four flavors are Ginger Ale, Cranberry Ginger Ale, Lime, and Black Cherry.

    How do the flavors differ?

    Ginger Ale is warm and citrusy. Cranberry Ginger Ale is tart. Lime is zesty and clean. Black Cherry is ripe and smooth. Every one of them is sparkling.

    Do you offer OEM or private label?

    Yes. Partners can shape a recipe with us, put their own name on the can, and have small batches filled and packed.

    What is in a can?

    Carbonated water, cane sugar, natural flavors, and citric acid. TzuSpark does not use artificial colors.
